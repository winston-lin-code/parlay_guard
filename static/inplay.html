    How prices and EV are shown

    Moneyline prices show recent best asks before fees. A price move compares the latest ask with the previous observed ask. EV uses depth for a $10 order, estimated fees, and a five-percentage-point probability stress test. NFL moneyline model probabilities account for the expected half payout on ties.

    Live EV is available for supported NBA and NFL regular-season markets. NFL live moneylines and totals cover the game phases that passed validation, up to the final two minutes. College, NFL, NHL, and MLB models cover supported pregame markets. Live scores and moneyline prices can continue for games without live EV.
