    How to read the estimates

    Avg. ask is the estimated average entry price for $10 of orders before fees. Open a row to see the best ask, fee estimate, net EV, and quote age.

    Screened EV applies fees and subtracts five percentage points from the model probability. The default view shows the highest positive screened EV per game as a paper pick. The five-point adjustment is a fixed stress test. Break-even probability uses the estimated fee and fill price.
