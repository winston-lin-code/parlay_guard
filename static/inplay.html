        How prices and EV are shown

        Moneyline prices show recent best asks before fees. A price move compares the latest ask with the previous observed ask. EV uses depth for a $10 order, estimated fees, and a five-percentage-point probability stress test. NFL moneyline model probabilities account for the expected half payout on ties.

        The halftime rule uses fresh Polymarket US moneyline depth for a $2 paper stake and subtracts five percentage points from model probability when calculating screened EV. It ranks one qualifying side per game by screened EV in leagues with a halftime moneyline model.

        Live EV is available for supported NBA, NFL, FBS football, and men’s Division I basketball markets. The college models cover validated regular-season markets and periods through two minutes remaining in regulation. College, NFL, NHL, and MLB models cover supported pregame markets. Live scores and moneyline prices can continue for games without live EV.
